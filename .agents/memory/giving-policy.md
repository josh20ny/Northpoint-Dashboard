---
name: Giving definitions and privacy
description: Giving visibility, giver definitions and approved Giving, Groups and Services development imports.
---

Everyone can see giving numbers broadly, but anything specific tied to a person
ID or name should only be visible to Admins.

**Why:** The owner explicitly clarified this during Phase 5.

**How to apply:** Separate broad totals from restricted donor transactions and
person-linked financial signals, including health inputs and exports.

Use General Giving throughout the main Giving trend. Show all-fund receipts
separately.

**Why:** When asked how workbook General Giving should connect to PCO's all-fund
totals, the owner chose “Use General Giving throughout”.

**How to apply:** Match the explicitly identified General Giving fund's
designations to accepted workbook General Giving. Do not include an entire
split gift in the main trend, add overlapping sources, or relabel all-fund
receipts as General Giving. This choice does not change the first-time/lapsed
giver definitions below, which concern gifts to Northpoint.

The owner's two key Giving measures are total dollars and the number of people
who give. Historical workbook giving-unit values are person counts, not money;
keep them distinct from dollar totals and from current PCO units that may group
explicit joint givers.

**Why:** The owner clarified that the important tracked measures are giving
total in dollars and giving units as the number of people who give.

**How to apply:** Preserve currency versus count and keep historical person-count
series separate from family-grouped PCO units unless their definitions align.

Keep yearly Fuel campaigns separate.

**Why:** The owner chose “Keep campaigns separate” rather than combining PCO's
year-labelled Fuel funds into the workbook's single Fuel category.

**How to apply:** Preserve each campaign's fund identity even when gifts arrive
in another calendar year. Do not assign undifferentiated workbook Fuel totals
to a campaign without explicit evidence.

The owner approved these category-to-PCO-fund matches: Strategic → NP Strategic;
Building → Building Fund - For Austin; Care → Care; Mosaic → MOSAIC; FOR →
FOR Fund. Care Outreach stays outside Care; Building and FOR stay separate.

**Why:** The owner selected “Use these matches” after reviewing those exact
matches and the stated exclusions.

**How to apply:** Keep these interpretations across current and historical
reporting. Other funds still contribute to all-fund receipts; do not broaden
the Care category or merge Building/FOR based on similar names.

A first-time giver is the first recorded giving event for that person/family at
Northpoint. A lapsed giver has a consistent giving record/cadence (weekly,
monthly, etc.) and then misses three consecutive expected gifts. A normally
monthly giver missing three consecutive months is lapsed.

**Why:** These are the owner's definitions; they also expect giving to feed the
health data in later phases.

**How to apply:** Preserve dates, evidence, person/family identity boundaries and
classification coverage for later health integration. Do not mistake a current
ingestion window's first transaction for the first recorded lifetime event, or
infer family membership from names/addresses. Consistency-detection details
must be distinguished from the owner-approved three-miss definition.

The owner accepted four consecutive gifts (three matching intervals) with up
to two days' date variation as the starting cadence rule, but explicitly said
this must be an Admin-adjusted formula whose definition can change later.

**Why:** “yes, but this should be an admin adjusted formula meaning we can change
its definition later if we would like”.

**How to apply:** Keep an Admin-only editable, versioned definition rather than
a fixed permanent constant. Retain the formula version with classifications
and future health inputs; never silently mutate historical evidence.

The owner approved current-data setup for Giving and Groups in this development
environment after disclosure of donor IDs, amounts, joint-giver relationships
and group memberships, with person-linked financial data Admin-only.

**Why:** The owner selected “Proceed with current-data setup” in response to
that explicit disclosure. Publishing and enabling unattended scheduling were
excluded from that approval.

**How to apply:** Do not repeatedly ask for the same development-data approval.
Actual PCO consent must still be completed by an authenticated Admin; chat
approval is not a provider grant or permission to impersonate an app user.
Leave existing scheduler settings unchanged unless separately authorized.

The owner also approved Services volunteer-assignment imports in development,
starting with the latest completed reporting week before historical backfills:
person IDs, team/service links, dates and assignment response statuses.

**Why:** The owner chose “Allow Services imports” after that scope and the
distinction between scheduled/accepted assignments and participation were
explained.

**How to apply:** Keep raw assignments Admin-only and publish aggregate counts
only after source interpretation. This does not authorize production publishing
or unattended scheduling; do not request the same development-data approval
again for these fields.
