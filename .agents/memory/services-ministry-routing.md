Map Waumba Land to Waumba Land, UpStreet and UpStreet Large Group to
UpStreet, Transit to Transit, and InsideOut to InsideOut.

Kids' Check-In serves both UpStreet and Waumba Land but is its own team.
LU Guest Services Weekly is Guest Services. Safety Team Schedule belongs
under Guest Services but remains its own team.

**Why:** The owner confirmed the ministry-specific matches and explicitly
identified the shared sources' meanings.

**How to apply:** Preserve these team distinctions in aggregate reporting.
Do not automatically duplicate shared Kids' Check-In volunteers into both
ministries or merge Safety into the other Guest Services team.

Report Kids' Check-In as a separate shared-team total, outside UpStreet and
Waumba Land ministry totals.

**Why:** The owner chose “Separate shared-team total” when asked explicitly
how this shared team's volunteers should count.

**How to apply:** Preserve the shared source identity instead of inventing a
ministry split or adding its full count to each children's ministry.

Sunday Morning maps to Service Programming (SPD).

**Why:** The owner subsequently selected “Confirm Service Programming”.

**How to apply:** Use this confirmed destination rather than treating Sunday
Morning as a church-wide volunteer total.

Transit Weekend is a separate reporting scope on Transit, not part of regular
Transit totals. Keep its scheduled/accepted response counts distinct from actual
participation.

**Why:** The owner chose “Keep separate” for the PCO Transit Weekend source.

**How to apply:** Route its records to the dedicated Transit Weekend scope and
preserve the distinction between schedule responses and attendance.

Historical ownership of these Services sources is not confirmed.

**Why:** When asked whether the same routes apply throughout their history,
the owner answered “I’m not sure”.

**How to apply:** Preserve the approved current routes, but do not silently
backdate them. Seek dated source evidence for earlier ownership; an old plan's
existence or a source's current name alone does not establish historical
volunteer counts or ministry ownership.

Phase 5B makes the ministries directory navigation-only. Show Guest Services in Connection directly below group attendance. Relocate shared Kids’ Check-In to Connection as a separately labeled team. Preserve dated team routing and avoid duplicating shared counts on the children’s pages.
