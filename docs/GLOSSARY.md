# Working Glossary

- **Week**: Mon–Sun local Chicago, dated by Sunday.
- **Actual**: observed count from a specified trusted source with coverage and revision.
- **Target / Plan**: leadership-entered goal for a period.
- **Statistical Forecast**: model estimate with uncertainty; not a goal.
- **Adult Online Connections**: provider-reported connections; may not equal people.
- **Sunday Total Engagement**: legacy aggregate attendance + scaled online connections; do not confuse with engagement tier.
- **Engaged N**: person meets at least N of four approved dimensions; inclusive/cumulative.
- **Exactly N**: person meets precisely N dimensions, mutually exclusive.
- **Sneak Peek**: observing a volunteer team before joining.
- **Discover Northpoint**: introductory group/event before joining next steps.
- **First-time gifts**: ambiguous: possible guest welcome gift (not a donation); requires owner definition.
- **Front door**: first identifiable connection with Northpoint; metric-specific.
- **Back door**: disengagement indicator; interval TBD.
- **Scorecard**: structured qualitative service evaluation, often 1–4.
- **Cohort maturity**: sufficient elapsed time for return/conversion measurement.
