# UX / Navigation / Interaction

## Information architecture
- Home: current week, rolling four-week attendance, YoY, giving summary, three decision questions, top anomalies, freshness and quick links.
- Ministry navigation: SPD, Giving, Connection, Communications, Waumba Land, UpStreet, Transit, InsideOut; Special Sundays and Camp 75.
- Analyze: time window/compare, engagement migration, journeys and attendance forecast.
- AI Analyst: conversation + highlighted evidence and safe share/export.
- Admin: integrations, users, metric rules, scorecards, manual entry, history import, alerts, audit.

## Interaction design
Every KPI clickable to metric definition, chart, ministry service/date breakdown, and source freshness. Comparison mode: actual/target/forecast; relative and absolute differences. Historical time shifts and missing data marked. Chart time-series consistent in Chicago local Sunday dates. Filters sticky only during session unless expressly saved.

## Accessibility
No color-only meaning; numerical labels and indicators; keyboard navigation, adequate contrast, mobile content order, clear zero vs missing, downloadable permission-safe tables and tooltips for ambiguous terminology.
