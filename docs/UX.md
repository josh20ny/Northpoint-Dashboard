# UX / Navigation / Interaction

## Information architecture
- Home uses the previous completed Sunday, prominent totals and compact ministry cards. Each has the selected weekly average, aligned YoY, a small YTD actual/eight-week-average chart and a direct ministry link. The [Phase 5B brief](PHASE_5B.md) selects the measures. Anomalies and forecasts belong to later phases.
- Ministry navigation: SPD, Giving, Connection, Communications, Waumba Land, UpStreet, Transit, InsideOut; Special Sundays and Camp 75.
- Analyze: time window/compare, engagement migration, journeys and attendance forecast.
- AI Analyst: conversation + highlighted evidence and safe share/export.
- Administration has collapsible navigation. Its landing page keeps user access, invitations, Check-In review and audit. Integrations and occasional historical Imports have separate routes. Future rule, scorecard and alert settings belong to their respective phases.

## Interaction design
Use the ministry directory for navigation and the detail page for service splits, dated history and drilldown. Put the average control beside the number and the historical date selector beside its table. Keep routine workbook/source labels and raw flags off staff views. Explain material partial or missing data briefly. Charts use Chicago reporting Sundays. A signed value or text accompanies color. See [Phase 5B](PHASE_5B.md) for the adult three-line chart and the child/student two-row layout. Target/forecast comparison follows the forecasting phase.

## Accessibility
No color-only meaning; numerical labels and indicators; keyboard navigation, adequate contrast, mobile content order, clear zero vs missing, downloadable permission-safe tables and tooltips for ambiguous terminology.
