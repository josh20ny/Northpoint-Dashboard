# Configurable Health and Next-step Scorecards

## Requirements
Admin configures metric membership, target source, directionality (higher/lower is better), green/yellow/red bands, weighting, minimum sample size, effective dates, and ministry applicability. Distinguish 'not configured'/'insufficient data' from red.

## Proposed default framework (NOT approved numeric values)
Leading: Discover attendance/conversion, Sneak Peek/serving conversion, group-interest→join, first-time kid/student returns, first-time welcome gifts, optional next step signals. Lagging: attendance vs plan, giving cadence, engagement level migration, membership/serving, baptisms. Do not blend incomparable units without transparent normalization/approved weighting.

## Change control
Save draft → preview historical impact (last 12/52 weeks) → approve with notes → effective date → recompute only affected periods. Store previous rule versions and who changed them. Show definition version and change marker in charts.

## Weekly triage
Health cards should explain `Current`, `Reference`, `Difference`, `Status`, `Reason`, `Data freshness`, `What changed`. AI can propose explanations but may not silently alter thresholds.
